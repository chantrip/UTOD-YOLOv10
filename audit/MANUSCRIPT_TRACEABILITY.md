## Related empirical interpretation

Original `main.tex` reports 5-run proposed means in the main table, but individual ablation rows are not shown with matched multi-seed confidence intervals. Therefore the manuscript should not read single-run per-factor ablation differences as strong causal evidence of component effects. The full three-factor crossed analysis is specified in [EXPERIMENT_MATRIX.md](EXPERIMENT_MATRIX.md) as a **future plan, not a published result**.

The reported robustness improvements on RUOD are promising hypotheses for follow-up, but their evidential value depends on whether the pristine RUOD test images or near duplicates influenced selection of architecture/checkpoints, and whether degradation subsets were fixed before evaluation. The original `eval.py` points to `ruod_bs_v2/weights/best.pt` and `ruod_dbw_v12/weights/best.pt` on a local filesystem; these checkpoints are not in the public source.

## Suggested submission-level stance when run data remain unavailable

If the CIoU baseline or split independence cannot be verified, **remove strong component-attribution language** and do not present purported untouched test AP for those runs. In particular, do not reuse old numeric ablations as evidence of isolated WIoU improvements just because the current scripts have `*_wiou` names. It is better to narrow to an exploratory reproducibility/robustness study with precise limitations than to assert an invalid controlled experiment.

**Note:** The source above is an experiment artifact and is not an official final CVC revision. Changes to the scientific manuscript should await a decision about which measured results can be defended; the audit branch itself adds no new measurements.
